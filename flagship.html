Rules of the Game

  1. Choose a level (Easy / Medium / Hard).
  2. Select a country from the list.
  3. Stage 1: Find the correct flag outline among the options. You have 5 attempts. Points: 5 on 1st try down to 1 on 5th try. Flags sharing the same geometric outline are accepted.
  4. Stage 2: Tap each region of the outline and choose the correct color from the palette. You have 5 attempts to get all colors right.
  5. Single player. Score accumulates across stages.
